## How to Use These Prompts

1. Open **Google AI Studio** (aistudio.google.com) or the Gemini API with Veo access.
2. Select the **Veo** video-generation model.
3. Paste each prompt individually and generate one clip at a time.
4. Set output duration to **7 seconds** per clip.
5. Download all three clips and import them into your video editor.
6. Arrange them in order: Clip 1 → Clip 2 → Clip 3.
7. Add a single continuous background music track across all three clips to reinforce continuity.
8. Export as a single 21-second vertical video (1080 × 1920) and upload to YouTube Shorts.

> **Tip:** If Veo lets you specify a seed or style reference, generate Clip 1 first, then use its style reference for Clips 2 and 3 to guarantee colour-grade consistency.
